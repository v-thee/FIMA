Rationale

Replacing Google Forms with a centralized platform

The Fund Request Management System automates and streamlines how student organizations request funds. Officers submit requests, upload documents and track progress, while COMMFIMA efficiently reviews submissions, manages approvals and keeps digital financial records.

  • Reduce manual administrative tasks
  • Organized, centralized digital records

Objectives

  1. 1

    A centralized platform for officers to submit fund requests and upload supporting documents electronically.

  2. 2

    Automate the review, approval and status monitoring so COMMFIMA can manage and track each submission.

  3. 3

    Improve document management and maintain organized digital records for transparency and accountability.

How it works

From request to release in five steps

  1. 01

    Sign in

    Use your authorized G Suite account and accept the Data Privacy Policy.

  2. 02

    Request fund

    Fill in the project details and submit your fund request.

  3. 03

    Upload documents

    Attach the approved resolution, signature and other requirements.

  4. 04

    COMMFIMA review

    Administrators review, add remarks and update the status.

  5. 05

    Track & record

    Get notified, track progress and keep records in the repository.
